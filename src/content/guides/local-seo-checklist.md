If you're a small business owner, there's a good chance you're also the marketing department. In BrightLocal's 2026 survey, 98% of small business owners said they're the ones making their own marketing decisions. So this checklist is for you: the owner with an afternoon, a laptop, and zero interest in jargon.

A couple who run a small frame shop in La Mesa did this entire checklist over two Sunday afternoons with a pot of coffee. They told me afterward it was the first time SEO made sense to them. Three months later, they were showing up on Google Maps for "custom framing near me" for the first time in the shop's 14-year history.

You can do this. Here's the list, in order.

## Part 1: See where you stand (15 minutes)

- [ ] Search your business name on Google from your phone. What shows up?
- [ ] Search "[your service] near me" and "[your service] in [your city]." Are you on the map? Where?
- [ ] Ask ChatGPT: "What's the best [your service] in [your city]?" Are you mentioned?
- [ ] Write down your top three competitors that keep showing up instead of you

Not showing up at all? Start with [Why Isn't My Business Showing Up on Google?](/business-not-showing-on-google)

## Part 2: Your Google Business Profile (45 minutes)

- [ ] Profile claimed and verified
- [ ] Business name exactly as on your sign, no extra keywords
- [ ] Most specific primary category, plus secondary categories
- [ ] Address, phone, and hours correct, including upcoming holiday hours
- [ ] Full description in plain language: what you do, who you serve, where
- [ ] Services listed
- [ ] At least 10 real photos: outside, inside, team, and work

Full version: [The Free Google Business Profile Checklist](/google-business-profile-checklist).

## Part 3: Reviews (20 minutes to set up, then a habit)

- [ ] Copy your Google review link
- [ ] Save a text message template for asking customers
- [ ] Decide who asks and when (right after the job, every time)
- [ ] Reply to every existing review you haven't answered

Scripts here: [How to Get More Google Reviews](/how-to-get-more-google-reviews).

## Part 4: Make your info match everywhere (30 minutes)

Pick one exact version of your name, address, and phone, then make it match on:

- [ ] Your website
- [ ] Apple Maps (Apple Business Connect)
- [ ] Bing Places
- [ ] Facebook and Instagram
- [ ] Yelp
- [ ] Your industry's top directories
- [ ] Your Chamber of Commerce

## Part 5: Your website (45 minutes)

- [ ] Homepage says, in words, what you do and where. Example: "Family-owned plumbing in Chula Vista and National City."
- [ ] Phone number is tappable on mobile
- [ ] Site loads quickly on your phone, on cellular data
- [ ] One page for each main service
- [ ] Each page has a clear title with your service and city
- [ ] A page answering the questions customers ask you most
- [ ] Your name, address, and phone in the footer

## Part 6: Show up in AI search (20 minutes)

- [ ] Your website answers common questions in plain sentences (pricing, hours, walk-ins, service area)
- [ ] Your reviews describe real experiences (ask customers to mention what you did for them)
- [ ] Check Reddit for your city plus your industry and see what people are saying
- [ ] Run the ChatGPT check from Part 1 again in a month

Full guide: [How to Get Your Business Recommended by ChatGPT](/get-business-recommended-by-chatgpt).

## Part 7: Your 15-minute monthly routine

- [ ] Reply to new reviews
- [ ] Add 2 to 3 new photos to your Google profile
- [ ] Post one or two updates on your Google profile
- [ ] Add or improve one page on your website
- [ ] Check that holiday hours are set

That's it. Fifteen minutes a month keeps you ahead of most of your competitors, because most of them aren't doing anything.

## When to stop DIYing

Doing it yourself works great for a lot of businesses. It might be time to get help if:

- You've done this whole list and still aren't showing up after 3 to 4 months
- Your Google profile is suspended and you can't get it back
- You're in a very competitive market, like law, dental, or home services in a big city
- Your time is worth more running the business than learning SEO

## FAQ

**Can I really do local SEO myself?**
Yes. Most of the basics, like your Google profile, reviews, listings, and simple website updates, are things owners can handle with a checklist and a few hours.

**What's the most important part of local SEO?**
For most local businesses, it's a complete, verified Google Business Profile plus a steady stream of recent reviews.

**How long does this checklist take?**
About 3 to 4 hours for the first pass, then about 15 minutes a month to maintain.

**Do I need to pay for any tools?**
No. Everything on this list can be done for free.
